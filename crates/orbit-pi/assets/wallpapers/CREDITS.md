# Built-in background credits

The images in this folder are the bundled backgrounds offered in
Settings → Appearance → Background. Each file is a 2560 px wide JPEG; `thumbs/`
holds 240 px copies for the Settings thumbnails.

Photos and illustrations from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) are used under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free to use, including
commercially, without permission; credit is not required but given here. The

The Japanese woodblock prints are 19th-century works in the public domain,
digitized by the museums named below and published on Unsplash.
